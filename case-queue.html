Design notes · My cases

  1. A personal case list, organized by stage, with the next action pulled from the checklist.
  2. Saved in a database on the private CM server and signed in with your Windows login. The whole CM team can view and edit, so handoffs and out-of-office coverage just work. IT/security to confirm data retention.
  3. Mine / Team / Unassigned views, reassign, and a change history on every case.
  4. Working vs not being worked (round 7): cases you're actively working show Resume (opens Work mode). Cases that are ready, waiting on the field, or out for delivery are greyed out and show View, which opens the clean read-only View mode. Try the Arya Stark case (ready to issue).
