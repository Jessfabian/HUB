Design notes

  1. A single entry point for everyone. New CMs click "Start MMSD case"; experienced CMs go straight to tools.
  2. Resume list of your saved cases (server-saved, team-shared). It shows profile pills and checklist progress.
  3. Quick tools open each tool on its own (standalone/expert use). The same tools also appear inside workstation steps.
  4. Shortcut: set the three biggest profile answers here and land in the workstation already filtered.
  5. Static MMSD service standards, shown as reminders (not live data).
  6. Until single sign-on: the CM picks themselves from the Working as selector (also in the top bar). Home then shows their cases, team, and pinned notes; generated emails use their signature.
  7. Personal stats come from the case timer (auto-starts when a case opens; see the timer in the top bar). Only you see your own numbers here, and nothing is ranked. Badges celebrate milestones.
  8. Optional kudos: teammates can thank each other. You can turn celebrations off in profile settings.
  9. Hero widget (round 6): pick one of four animated widgets with the chooser: live weather (date, time, and an animated sky), pet Questy, a breathing break, or pop-the-NIGO bubbles. Your choice is remembered.
  10. All sample cases use made-up Game of Thrones names, fake policy numbers, and silly emails.
