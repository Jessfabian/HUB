Design notes · Sign-in (no SSO needed)

  1. Our own login runs inside the Flask app on the private server: work email + password. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash (never readable), sessions use secure cookies, and the site requires HTTPS. If SSO ever comes, accounts are keyed by email, so we can switch over without losing any cases, settings, or timer history.
  2. No self sign-up. A manager or admin invites people from the roster (User accounts). Each person gets a one-time link, sets a password, then lands on Profile & settings. Their role (CM, AM, manager, admin) comes from the invite. The "Working as" picker goes away; the app knows who you are.
  3. Safety: lockout after 5 failed tries, auto sign-out after 30 minutes idle (with a 2-minute warning, and your case is autosaved), "keep me signed in" up to 8 hours, and every sign-in logged.
  4. Optional, to ask IT: the server can also use Windows sign-in (IIS / Kerberos), so people on the MassMutual network are signed in automatically with no password. This is not a full SSO project. Our login page stays as the fallback.
