Design notes · My wins (morale)

  1. Using the site is optional, so it should feel rewarding. This page is private to the CM. There are no leaderboards and no ranking against teammates.
  2. Badges for milestones and for learning: first trust case, first joint policy, each new skill trained, streaks, kudos. Locked badges hint at what to try next. The list can be edited by managers.
  3. Personal trend from the case timer. It compares you only to your own past times and breaks it down by case type, so newly trained skills show progress.
  4. A small celebration appears when "Mark IR complete" is clicked in the workstation. It is subtle, closes itself after a few seconds, and can be turned off.
  5. Peer kudos: teammates are picked from the roster. Kudos also appear on Home. Managers can see kudos counts (not the content) in Team insights if the team agrees.
