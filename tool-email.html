Design notes · Email builder

  1. Templates are grouped by stage. The firm/agency number drives recipients and wording (e.g. EDJ NB contact only).
  2. Recipients come from a simple firm contact list that CMs can edit. The account manager is linked to the firm/agency number (still to be decided; it can be typed in for now). Required forms attach automatically from the requirements and can be removed or added.
  3. Open draft in Outlook downloads a ready-to-send email file with To/CC, subject, body, and the PDFs attached. In classic Outlook it opens as an editable draft; you review it, then click Send. For the new Outlook, where this may open read-only, Copy email + download forms copies the body and downloads the PDFs so you can paste and attach. The server keeps copies of the common forms so attaching is fast and works offline.
