Design notes · User accounts

  1. Replaces the "Working as" picker. Managers and admins invite people from the private roster; nobody can create their own account.
  2. Status flow: Not invited → Invited (one-time link, 72 h) → Active. Lockouts and disabled accounts are shown clearly with a one-click fix. Passwords are never visible to anyone, including admins.
  3. Roles control which pages show in the sidebar and what each person can edit. The server enforces them (not only hidden in the page).
  4. When someone leaves the roster their account is disabled automatically, and their open cases go to their manager to reassign.
