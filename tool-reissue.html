Design notes · Reissue tool

  1. Standalone tool for CMs and account managers (AMs handle most reissues). It uses the same engine as the workstation's "Change after approval" button. Open a saved case and everything pre-fills.
  2. Three paths, following the MMSD AM & CM Reissue Processing topic:
    • Change after approval: REDESIGN.
    • 90-day reissue: F6126 checklist (MMSD + agency #, PAC note, illustration if the premium changes, new HIPAA if the DOB changes) sent to the reissue board.
    • HO error/internal: no F6126; amendments, revised illustration, Supplement to Application, and the HO Error/Correction email to the correct internal board.
    Internal board addresses are shown to staff only, never put in field emails.
  3. Before/after can be pre-filled from two illustrations. Only changed rows are highlighted and sent to the referral check.
  4. The referral check is driven by the Quest tables (non-NY / NY). It shows UW and suitability separately, with the reason for each "Yes".
  5. Fills your two Outlook templates field by field, plus F6126 and HO Error templates. Subject prefix (Redesign / Reissue / PL), Urgent flag, and the concurrent policy list are added automatically.
