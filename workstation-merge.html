Design notes · Option 1 (Merged)

  1. One page per case. The case header pills summarize the profile at a glance. The existing IR workbench's data-entry panels (case setup, state requirements, TreX review, BINGO, review output) are rebuilt as steps here, and the old workbench page is retired.
  2. Guided shows the "why" text and the full sub-checks. Expert hides the explanations and leaves compact checkboxes plus tool buttons. Try the toggle.
  3. Profile questions drive everything. Gold-edged fields appear only when relevant; each answer instantly adds or removes steps, watch-outs, and templates.
  4. Stages run IR → Interim → Issue → Delivery. Pre-IR is excluded (separate team). The chevron path shows the phases within the stage.
  5. Gold-edged steps are conditional. They appear only because of a profile answer, and the pill says which one. A BOT-reviewed eSigned case would instead show "Act on CaMPIR BOT note" and hide the manual requirement sweep.
  6. Tools can be embedded inside a step or opened as a mini panel, with no page change. Save Age shown: because the application requests Save Age, it uses the DOB and Part 1 signature date from the dropped application and calculates automatically. Outputs flow to the case drawer.
  7. The right rail only lists items triggered by this profile.
  8. The case output drawer gathers requirements, amendments, the IR note, and calculations into one copy-ready package and email (replaces today's Review Output Package). ✉ Send email opens a composer right on the page (like Salesforce): template, To (firm contact only), CC (account manager), subject, body, and the required form PDFs already attached. Click Send and it goes out from your own mailbox. "Open in Outlook" stays as a backup.
  9. Saved on the server (database on the same private server). The whole CM team can view and edit, which supports handoffs. It autosaves and shows the owner and who last edited, plus a change history. Case type was removed (always new business).
  10. Drop in documents: the Part 1 application and illustration are read, including scanned ones (this builds on the existing illustration validator). Fields are pre-filled: product, state, owner, replacement, 1035, payment/PAC, Save Age request, DOB, and Part 1 date. Each one shows a "from app p.X · verify" tag until the CM confirms it.
  11. Quest links: only pages relevant to this profile are listed. In Guided, each link sits on its step next to the note. In Expert, links leave the steps and appear all together in this box, grouped by stage. Try the toggle.
  12. Every requirement is mapped to its form(s). Adding a requirement automatically adds the correct (state) form to the email attachments.
  13. Linked policies: link any companion. Same insured (e.g. WL + Term): one combined IR email listing all policy numbers, saved in each case. Family (different insureds): linked so you can see them and they show in the concurrent-policies list, but each gets its own email.
  14. Policy design is read from the illustration. Change after approval opens a before/after table. The referral check applies the Quest UW Referral Table (non-NY) or the UW & Reg 187 table (NY: UW and/or LifeBA suitability). It lists the system requirements to add (REDESIGN / HOLD) and fills the right template: "Change Request After Underwriter Approval", or the NY "Change after UW Approval/Reissue" template with F5436NY(L). It then opens an Urgent Outlook draft. When approval comes back, one click applies the new design and starts the amendments.
  15. The referral check covers face increases (Underwriter Note pre-approval), rider adds (WP pre-approval), plan changes (more or less than $1,000 premium change), reinsurance exceptions, owner/beneficiary/assignment changes, 1035 changes, and NY suitability (premium, risk class, gender, age change). Unclear cases say "check Quest" instead of guessing.
  16. Internal notes are optional, timestamped, signed with the CM's name, visible to the team, and never copied into emails. Pinned notes show at the top of the case and in My cases.
  17. Case timer (management request). It starts automatically when the case opens and keeps time per checklist stage. Pause asks for a quick reason. It auto-pauses when idle, and the CM can correct a time with a reason. Times are saved with the case on the server and feed Team insights (managers) and My wins (the CM's own private stats). Try Pause.
  18. Mark IR complete stops the timer, moves the case to Interim, and shows a small celebration (closes itself; can be turned off in settings). Try it.
  19. Send email from the case (round 6): pick a template and it fills in the policy, insured, requirements, and your signature. Required forms attach automatically, and you can add more from the forms library or case documents. Sending saves a copy to Case activity and your Outlook Sent Items, and marks the requirements "Requested · today". Replies show up in your Outlook as usual. Behind the scenes the server sends through MassMutual's mail system as you; IT must approve this (Microsoft Graph or the internal mail relay).
  20. Work mode vs View mode (round 7): ✎ Work mode is the full checklist. Each section (the case profile and every step) has 💾 Save and ✔ Done; Done locks it so a stray click can't change anything, and a tiny ✎ pencil unlocks it. Mark IR complete locks the whole case and switches to 👁 View mode: a clean, read-only summary (status, key facts, requirements, activity). Click ✎ Work on this case to go back; it asks "are you sure?" first and the edit is logged in History.
